One funny line of text, written for make.fun's "Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply" bounty.

## The line

> I'm out of office until Monday. My inbox is being watched by my cat, who has already deleted everything important.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It is a real away message first and a joke second: the owner is out until Monday, and the inbox has a watcher — a cat who has already deleted everything important. The turn is fresh (the cover is worse than the absence), the laugh lands on the first read with no setup and nothing to explain, and it stands alone with no signature or name. Short enough to take in at a glance, with every word earning its place. One line, 114 characters, inside the 160-character gate.
